# Data Visualization Project

## Project Overview
This project analyzes a Superstore sales dataset using Python and creates visualizations to understand sales and profit patterns.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- VS Code

## Visualizations Created
1. Sales by Category
2. Profit by Category
3. Sales by Region
4. Sales by Customer Segment

## Dataset
The project uses the `superstore.csv` dataset.

## How to Run
1. Install Python.
2. Install the required libraries: `pandas` and `matplotlib`.
3. Keep `superstore.csv` and `visualization.py` in the same folder.
4. Run `python visualization.py` in the terminal.

## Conclusion
The visualizations help identify sales and profit patterns across product categories, regions, and customer segments.

## Key Insights and Business Recommendations

### Key Insights
- Sales by category helps identify which product categories generate the most revenue.
- Profit by category helps compare the profitability of different product categories.
- Sales by region highlights differences in sales performance across regions.
- Sales by customer segment shows which customer groups contribute the most sales.

### Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing efforts on product categories with strong sales and profit.
- Investigate categories with high sales but relatively low profit to identify ways to reduce costs.
- Compare regional performance to identify opportunities for business growth.
- Develop targeted offers and marketing strategies for different customer segments.

### Dashboard
The project includes a combined dashboard named `superstore_dashboard.png`, which presents four charts in one view for easier comparison and decision-making.

### Conclusion
This project uses Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib to transform raw Superstore data into visual insights that can support business decisions.
## Actual Findings from the Dataset

### 1. Sales by Category
- Technology generated the highest sales: 836,154.03.
- Furniture generated sales of 741,999.80.
- Office Supplies generated sales of 719,047.03.

### 2. Profit by Category
- Technology generated the highest profit: 145,454.95.
- Office Supplies generated a profit of 122,490.80.
- Furniture generated the lowest profit: 18,451.27, despite having the second-highest sales.

### 3. Sales by Region
- The West region recorded the highest sales: 725,457.82.
- The East region recorded sales of 678,781.24.
- The South region recorded the lowest sales: 391,721.90.

### 4. Sales by Customer Segment
- Consumer customers contributed the highest sales: 1,161,401.34.
- Corporate customers generated sales of 706,146.37.
- Home Office customers generated sales of 429,653.15.

### Business Recommendations
- Investigate furniture costs, discounts, and product-level profitability to understand its comparatively low profit.
- Analyze successful sales strategies in the West region and assess whether they could work in other regions.
- Maintain customer-focused strategies for the Consumer segment while exploring growth opportunities in Corporate and Home Office segments.
- Continue monitoring sales and profit together to support better business decisions.

**Note:** The dataset's currency symbol is not specified here, so the figures are presented without a currency label.
